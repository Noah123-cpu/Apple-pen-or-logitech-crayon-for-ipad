# Why many styluses don’t work well on iPhone

## Capacitive touch basics
iPhones use capacitive touch designed for finger input.

## Generic stylus = “finger substitute”
Cheap rubber/mesh styluses work like a finger:
- no palm rejection
- no pressure
- no tilt
- less precision

## Pencil-style performance needs platform support
Apple Pencil-style performance relies on iPad hardware/software support that iPhones generally don’t provide.
