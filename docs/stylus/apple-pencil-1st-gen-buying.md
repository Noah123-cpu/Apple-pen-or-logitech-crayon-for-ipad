# Apple Pencil (1st gen) buying notes

## Why it’s best for art
- Best integration with iPad
- Palm rejection + tilt + pressure sensitivity (app dependent)

## Where to look
- Local marketplace (best chance to hit $40 CAD)
- eBay (watch shipping + condition)

## Avoid traps
- "for parts", "untested", "won’t charge"
- sellers who won’t confirm pairing/charging

## Seller checklist (ask for proof)
- It charges
- It pairs via Bluetooth with an iPad
- Tip condition (tips are replaceable)
- Cap/adapter included (nice, not required)
