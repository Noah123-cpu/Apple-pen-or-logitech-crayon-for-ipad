# Logitech Crayon buying notes

## Why it’s a strong budget pick
- Reliable, good palm rejection, low lag
- Great for notes + drawing
- Often cheaper than Apple Pencil used

## Tradeoff vs Apple Pencil
- Usually no pressure sensitivity
- Still excellent for many art styles

## Where to look
- Local marketplace (best for $40 CAD)
- Used/refurb electronics shops
- Online listings (watch shipping)
