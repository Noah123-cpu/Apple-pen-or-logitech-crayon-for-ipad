# iPad (8th generation) stylus compatibility (Lightning)

## Best “works-for-years” options
- Apple Pencil (1st generation) — official best match for iPad 8th gen.
- Logitech Crayon — very reliable “made for iPad” alternative.

## Avoid for iPad 8th gen
- Apple Pencil (2nd gen) — not compatible with this iPad model.
- USB-C-only pencils — usually intended for USB-C iPads; your iPad is Lightning.

## Budget reality (CAD)
- Apple Pencil 1st gen often costs more than $40 CAD used once shipping is included.
- Logitech Crayon used can sometimes hit ~$40 CAD locally.
